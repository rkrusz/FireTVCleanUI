# Bundled scenic backgrounds

The eight `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/scenic_*.webp` images were generated specifically for this project with OpenAI's image generator on October 22, 2026. No CleanFrame images, stock photographs, user photos, TV serial numbers, account details, or third-party logos were used.

Scenes: mountain lake, coast, autumn forest, desert, starry alpine peaks, rolling hills, waterfall, winter lake. Source generations were 1672×941 PNGs; the committed assets were center-cropped to 16:9, resized to 1920×1080 and compressed as WebP quality 78 using Pillow. The combined WebP size at preparation was 1,560,506 bytes. For reproducible replacements, create new licensed/original landscapes, fit each to 1920×1080 and save as WebP in `drawable-nodpi` with the same resource names. The generation PNGs are not needed to build the app and are not in this repository.

These are synthetic images, not photographs of real locations. They may have small AI-generated visual imperfections. The entire app works offline and makes no image-service requests.

`app_icon.png` and `tv_banner.png` are derived from the generated mountain scene, with an added title for the TV launcher. They are not CleanFrame branding.
